01 — HOW IT WORKS

From moving air to clean power.

Wind turbines use the movement of air to rotate blades and generate electricity.

01

Wind Moves

Moving air creates kinetic energy that can be captured by turbines.

02

Blades Rotate

Wind pushes the turbine blades and makes them spin.

03

Generator Works

Mechanical movement drives a generator to produce electricity.

04

Power Delivered

Generated electricity can be supplied to homes and businesses.

02 — TURBINE ANATOMY

Meet the parts behind the power.

Every part of a wind turbine has an important role in converting wind into energy.

01

Rotor Blades

Large blades capture energy from moving wind.

02

Nacelle

The housing at the top contains important mechanical components.

03

Tower

The tower supports the turbine and places it higher into the wind.

04

Generator

The generator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
